Name: Glytora  
Generic Name: Hexafluronide  
Drug Class: Glucose Transport Modulator (GTM)

---

Indications and Usage:
Glytora is indicated as an adjunct to diet and exercise to improve glycemic control in adults with type 2 diabetes mellitus.  

It may be used as:
- Monotherapy in newly diagnosed patients  
- Dual therapy in combination with metformin or GLP-1 agonists  
- Add-on therapy for patients not controlled on insulin regimens

Glytora is not indicated for treatment of type 1 diabetes or diabetic ketoacidosis.

---

Dosage and Administration:
Glytora is taken orally once daily with the first main meal of the day.  

Initial dosing:
- 100mg once daily, regardless of renal function

Titration:
- Increase to 200mg after 4 weeks if glycemic targets are not met  
- Maximum dose: 300mg/day

Tablets should be swallowed whole. Do not split or crush.

---

Side Effects:
Common side effects (≥5% of patients in trials):
- Gastrointestinal upset (nausea, bloating)
- Mild fatigue or drowsiness
- Metallic taste
- Increased thirst in first 7–10 days

Rare but serious side effects:
- Lactic acidosis (in cases of overdose)
- Hypoglycemia when used with insulin
- Bladder irritation

---

Warnings and Precautions:
- Periodic monitoring of renal function (eGFR) is advised, especially in elderly patients
- Use caution in patients with known GI conditions (e.g., IBS, Crohn’s)
- May mask early signs of hypoglycemia when used with sulfonylureas or insulin
- Avoid alcohol consumption during treatment

---

Contraindications:
Do not use Glytora in:
- Type 1 diabetes
- Patients with severe renal impairment (eGFR < 30 mL/min/1.73m²)
- Patients with active bladder cancer
- Pregnant or breastfeeding individuals (not studied in this population)
- Known hypersensitivity to Hexafluronide or related compounds

---

Mechanism of Action:
Glytora selectively binds to GLUT-7 transport proteins in intestinal epithelial cells, reducing postprandial glucose absorption without altering insulin secretion.

By modulating SGLT and GLUT activity, Glytora lowers the glucose spike after meals while preserving endogenous insulin response. It has minimal impact on hepatic gluconeogenesis.

---

Formulations and Strengths:
Glytora is available as immediate-release tablets:
- 100 mg (pale green, hexagonal)
- 200 mg (white, oval)
- 300 mg (light blue, round)

Each tablet is film-coated and scored for accurate dosing.

---

Storage and Handling:
- Store at 15–30°C (59–86°F)
- Do not refrigerate or freeze
- Protect from excessive humidity
- Keep in original blister pack to preserve potency

---

---

How It Works:
Glytora works by delaying and reducing the absorption of dietary glucose at the intestinal level. Unlike DPP-4 or SGLT-2 inhibitors, it acts directly on membrane transporters, providing consistent glycemic control without affecting sodium or insulin pathways.

In clinical trials (N=2800), patients on Glytora showed:
- A1C reduction of 1.3% at 12 weeks
- 82% improved fasting glucose levels
- 72% experienced fewer post-meal glucose spikes
