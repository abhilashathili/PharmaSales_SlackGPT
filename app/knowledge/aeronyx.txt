Name: Aeronyx  
Generic Name: Lumacortisol  
Drug Class: Inhaled Corticosteroid + Long-Acting Bronchodilator (ICS-LABA)

---

Indications and Usage:
Aeronyx is indicated for:
- Long-term maintenance treatment of asthma in patients aged 12 and older
- Prevention of bronchospasm in patients with moderate to severe chronic asthma
- Adjunctive therapy for patients with exercise-induced bronchoconstriction (EIB)

Aeronyx is not indicated for:
- Acute bronchospasm relief
- Primary therapy in status asthmaticus

Use in children under 12 has not been established.

---

Dosage and Administration:
Aeronyx is administered via oral inhalation using a pre-metered inhaler.

Standard dosage:
- Maintenance: 1 inhalation (125 mcg/50 mcg) twice daily, approximately 12 hours apart
- For patients with inadequate control: may increase to 250 mcg/50 mcg twice daily, as tolerated

Administration instructions:
- Shake inhaler well before use
- Rinse mouth with water (do not swallow) after each dose to reduce risk of oropharyngeal candidiasis
- Discard inhaler after 60 actuations

---

Side Effects:
Common adverse reactions (≥5%):
- Hoarseness or voice changes
- Oral thrush (fungal infection)
- Dry mouth
- Headache
- Nervousness or tremors

Less common but serious:
- Paradoxical bronchospasm
- Hypokalemia (low potassium)
- Adrenal suppression with long-term use

---

Warnings and Precautions:
- Do not use Aeronyx to treat sudden asthma attacks
- Risk of systemic corticosteroid effects exists, particularly with high doses or prolonged use
- Monitor growth in pediatric patients during prolonged therapy
- May increase risk of pneumonia in patients with chronic respiratory disease

Patients should be assessed regularly for risk of adrenal insufficiency, bone density loss, and visual changes (e.g., cataracts, glaucoma).

---

Contraindications:
- Aeronyx is contraindicated in patients with:
  - Known hypersensitivity to Lumacortisol or excipients
  - Primary treatment of acute bronchospasm
  - History of severe milk protein allergy (lactose-based propellant used)

---

Mechanism of Action:
Aeronyx combines two active ingredients:
1. **Lumacortisol** – a synthetic corticosteroid that reduces airway inflammation, mucus production, and bronchial hyperreactivity.
2. **Formoteracline** – a long-acting beta-2 agonist (LABA) that relaxes smooth muscles in the bronchial walls for up to 12 hours.

Together, they provide both symptom prevention and long-term control of airway inflammation.

---

Formulations and Strengths:
Each metered dose contains:
- 125 mcg Lumacortisol + 50 mcg Formoteracline (standard strength)
- 250 mcg Lumacortisol + 50 mcg Formoteracline (high strength)

The inhaler delivers 60 pre-metered doses and includes a dose counter.

---

Storage and Handling:
- Store at 20–25°C (68–77°F)
- Do not freeze or expose to temperatures above 50°C
- Avoid direct sunlight and heat sources
- Replace cap tightly after each use
- Discard 30 days after opening foil pouch

---

How It Works:
Aeronyx’s dual-mechanism approach:
- **Reduces inflammation** in bronchial tissue through local steroid activity
- **Improves airway patency** through sustained bronchodilation

This reduces asthma flare-ups, improves lung function (FEV1), and enables symptom-free periods with fewer rescue inhaler uses.

In pivotal trials (N=3600), Aeronyx reduced severe exacerbation risk by 42% and improved peak expiratory flow (PEF) by 18% within 8 weeks.
